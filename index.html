🔬 INTRODUCTION TO GENE EDITING

Gene editing is a concept of modern science including the alternation of dna for benefits. This includes changing human, animal and crop's genes, and is continuosly developing as a scientific technique

🧬 HOW IT WORKS: CRISPR-CAS9

Scientists use the CRISPR system as a targeting mechanism in genetic engineering.Cas9 can cut DNA. Once guided to the location Cas9 makes a precise cut in the DNA double helix. The cell then repairs this cut, and scientists can use that repair process to alter the gene.
